# AWS Certification Learning

> A **beginner-friendly study site for AWS certifications**. The goal is not just to pass — but to truly understand and be able to apply it.

This site is designed for people who have **never touched AWS, or even the cloud**. For every topic we follow three principles:

1. **Start with "why you need it"** — use real-life examples so you understand the actual problem each service solves.
2. **Always explain the jargon first** — we assume you know nothing; every acronym comes with its full name and a plain-language explanation.
3. **Certification + real-world skills together** — every page has "hands-on", "real scenarios", "common mistakes", and "cost tips". Pass the exam, and use it at work.

## How to use this site

- 📘 **Study notes** — knowledge points organized by official exam domain, with plain explanations, diagrams, and analogies.
- ⚡ **Flashcards** — click to flip and review quickly before the exam.
- ✍️ **Original practice questions** — in the style of the official exam; answer first, then see the detailed explanation.
- 📅 **Exam info** — registration, fees, question types, passing score, and a study plan.
